# Battle models

## Current realistic battle pass

- `solus-knight.glb`: **Solus the Knight**, Manoel de Oliveira (ManNeko).
  Source and express permission to use in games/projects: https://manneko.itch.io/solustheknight
  Downloaded free October 4, 2026. Converted from the creator's embedded glTF to GLB without changing the mesh or animation data.
  Original character and animations are by ManNeko, not Fairy World. Separately rendered weapon geometry is original project code.
- `creatures/dragon.glb`: **Cethiel's Dragon**, Cethiel and Drummyfish, CC0 1.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/cethiels-dragon-3d
  Re-exported October 4, 2026 with repaired action sampling and retimed idle, walk, attack and death clips.
- `terrain/forest-floor-*.jpg`: **Forest Floor**, eye-candy.xyz / Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor — https://polyhaven.com/license
  Original 1K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, downloaded October 5, 2026.
